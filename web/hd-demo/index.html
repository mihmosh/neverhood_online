I / The scenery

A promising new surface

The backgrounds were reworked with Gemini, with promising results. The aim is to explore more detail without losing the handmade strangeness of the original rooms.

II / The moving pieces

Detail needs continuity

Sprites use RealESRNet_x4plus at 4×. Upscaling frame by frame is not optimal: textures can flicker between frames. A future animation or video temporal upscale could add detail with better consistency and reduced texture flicker.

III / A small, sculpted detail

A cursor made of clay

The cursor was sculpted and rebuilt in Blender, then rendered into sprite phases. Only some states are covered; other states use the native cursor.

IV / Under the surface

Original logic, experimental rendering

ScummVM's WebAssembly build runs the original game logic; a JavaScript rendering proxy supplies the HD visuals. Animation currently has slight stutters. GPU-side optimization or native HD asset support in ScummVM are possible future work, not proven fixes.
